FAQ

Useful answers before a sales call.

If the fit is wrong, the answer should become clear early—not after a long proposal process.

Ask a different question ↗
How long does SEO take?+

The first 90 days are an implementation cycle, not a ranking promise. Competitive search growth usually requires sustained work beyond the first quarter.

Do you guarantee rankings?+

No. Serphic guarantees transparent priorities, agreed implementation standards, ethical work and client ownership—not a Google position nobody controls.

Do you work with one-location companies?+

Yes. Core is designed for established single-location plumbing or HVAC operators when the market, capacity and opportunity fit the scope.

Do you create city pages?+

Only where a page serves a real market need and can offer distinct value. Serphic does not build thin city-swapped doorway networks.

Will I own my website, content and accounts?+

Yes. Clients retain ownership of their website, Google Business Profile, analytics, content and other digital assets.

What about AI search visibility?+

We monitor it and structure the site so strong search fundamentals carry into AI discovery. It supports the strategy; it does not replace Google Search and Maps for home services.
